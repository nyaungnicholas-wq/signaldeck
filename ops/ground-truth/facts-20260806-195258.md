## CRUX — independent sample size, not row count

The row count is NOT the sample size. Roughly a thousand symbols share one
market move per day, so trade-level n overstates independence by one to two
orders of magnitude. Never quote an accuracy or return figure without the
distinct-day count beside it. (2026-08-06: 165,456 rows were 33 days.)
```
INDEPENDENT   33 days, mean -48.36 bps/day, profitable 13/33
trade-level   n=165456, gross -18.17 bps  <- NOT independent, do not quote alone
```
