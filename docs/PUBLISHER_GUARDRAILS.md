# Publisher guardrails

SignalDeck's member product is meant to stay on the publisher side of the line
between publishing research and giving personalised investment advice. These
are the rules it keeps, and what enforces each one. Changing a rule means
changing its test in the same commit, with the reason.

Not legal advice; counsel review pending. This file records engineering
controls, not a legal conclusion.

| # | Rule | Enforced by |
|---|------|-------------|
| 1 | **Impersonal.** Every member reads the same forecasts. Nothing a member reads about a symbol depends on who they are, what else they watch, or their settings. | `TestMemberForecastsAreImpersonal` (daemon/internal/api/guardrails_test.go): two members with different watchlists and alert settings get byte-identical bodies from every member-reachable GET, except the listed per-user routes (own watchlist, own identity, own alert switches). `TestComposeIsImpersonal` (daemon/internal/memberdigest) does the same for the daily email. |
| 2 | **Same forecasts for all, including in email.** The daily read is a filtered view of the public forecast table, worded identically for everyone, and says so in its footer. | `memberdigest.Compose` builds each symbol's block from the symbol and the shared forecast rows only; footer text pinned by `TestComposeFlipsCryptoAndFooter`. |
| 3 | **No account or portfolio inputs.** No member route accepts capital, position size, portfolio, holdings, risk tolerance, balance, equity, quantity or leverage. | `TestMemberRoutesTakeNoPersonalInputs` (guardrails_test.go): a source scan of every handler a member session can reach, pinned to an allowlist (`memberInputs`); a parameter whose name matches those words fails outright, an unlisted one fails until listed. |
| 4 | **No personal sizing.** Members are never told how much to buy or sell. | Rule 3 removes the inputs sizing would need; position, portfolio and paper-order routes are admin-only (`memberRoutes` allowlist in daemon/internal/api/accounts.go; `TestMemberSurfaceIsPinned`). |
| 5 | **No execution for members.** Members cannot place, route or simulate orders, and nothing they do starts ingestion. | Member tier gate (security.go step 6b, `memberAllowed`); `neverMember` list and `TestMemberRoutesServeNoLicensedData` (membersurface_test.go) keep subscribe/unsubscribe and order paths off the member surface. |
| 6 | **Hypothetical-performance labels.** Any backtest or paper number shown to a member says it is hypothetical and not a promise of results. | `TestMemberForecastsAreImpersonal` also requires every `historicalAccuracy` on the member's `/api/regimes` and `/api/vol-regime` to carry `"evidence":"backtest"` (stored vol forecasts lost that label until `volregime.Labelled`); the email footer says "backtest accuracy is hypothetical and not a promise of results" (`TestComposeFlipsCryptoAndFooter`). |
| 7 | **Describe the model literally.** The model is statistical classifiers trained on price and volume history. It is not an AI adviser and is never described as one. | Email footer line pinned by `TestComposeFlipsCryptoAndFooter`; the LLM summary never runs for a member (`/api/company/profile` member branch, membersurface_test.go). |
| 8 | **Derived data only; no crypto for members.** Members see forecasts, never vendor prices, volumes, returns or headlines; crypto is not part of the member product. | `TestMemberResponsesCarryNoVendorSentinels` (membersentinel_test.go) for every member route; `TestDigestCarriesNoVendorSentinels` for the daily email. |
| 9 | **Opt-in delivery.** Nothing is emailed or messaged unless the member turned it on; every email carries a one-click unsubscribe (a GET only shows a confirm button, so link scanners cannot opt anyone out; RFC 8058 List-Unsubscribe headers for mail clients). Email links use the configured public URL only. | `member_alert_prefs` defaults off (`TestMemberAlertPrefs`); `TestUnsubscribeLinkIsAnonymousAndTokened`; `TestWorkerSendsListUnsubscribeHeaders`; `TestDigestMailNeedsConfiguredPublicURL`; `TestWorkerSendsOncePerDayAndHonoursUnsubscribe`. |
| 10 | **The journal grades the member, never the forecast.** A member's call journal (/journal) records the member's OWN up/down calls and grades them by the rules SignalDeck grades itself with; it never changes, reorders or personalises SignalDeck's forecasts, and it shows the grade only (no entry or exit price, no return). | Journal routes are per-user (`perUserRoutes`) and their inputs are listed in `memberInputs` as the member's own calls; `TestMemberResponsesCarryNoVendorSentinels` and `TestJournalNeverServesPrices` hold the no-price line; `TestJournalFeedsNoForecast` (internal/memberjournal) fails if any file outside the journal's own names its table, rows or package. |
| 11 | **The risk headline is automatic and verdict-driven.** /today's "Risk first" block (plan step 9) promotes the HAR volatility forecast to the member headline only when the live record (`/api/vol-forecast/record`) publishes the pre-registered pass verdict `BEATS THE NULLS` for the headline cell, horizon 1. `NO SKILL DEMONSTRATED` or `ESTIMATOR ARTIFACT` shows an honest did-not-beat strip; `INSUFFICIENT` or `ACCRUING` a days-of-60 progress strip; anything else says the record is unavailable and claims nothing. Nobody flips it by hand and the web never re-derives a verdict from the numbers. As of 2026-10-02 the record emits only `INSUFFICIENT` or `ACCRUING`: the pre-registered grader does not yet write its verdict into it, so the pass and fail states stay dormant until it does. Per-symbol forecasts reach members only through `/api/vol-forecast/latest`, and the SERVER gates that route on the same verdict: unless the record's horizon-1 verdict is exactly `BEATS THE NULLS` it answers `{"available": false, "reason": ...}` with no forecast in it, so no client can show an unvalidated number by skipping the web's gate. When served: symbol, horizon, date, annualised vol; no null, coefficient or outcome. | `web/src/lib/riskHeadline.test.mjs` (all states, horizon-5-alone and winning-numbers-without-verdict do not promote, malformed payloads); `TestVolForecastLatestServesOnlyTodaysDerivedForecasts`; `TestVolForecastLatestIsGatedOnTheVerdict` (INSUFFICIENT, ACCRUING, NO SKILL, ESTIMATOR ARTIFACT and a wrong-case pass all serve no forecast); the route is probed by `TestMemberResponsesCarryNoVendorSentinels` and compared by `TestMemberForecastsAreImpersonal`. |
| 12 | **Ask the data reports records, never advice.** /ask (plan step 10) answers one question at a time from SignalDeck's own tables only, through a fixed catalog of read-only queries (the model never writes SQL), and every sentence cites a returned row or the answer is replaced by the rows; the one uncited sentence allowed is the exact closing "The rows do not answer that.". Members are off by default (`SIGNALDECK_MEMBER_COPILOT`), and when on they get only derived or public-domain catalog entries, the same rows for the same question except their OWN journal counts; raw live records that skip the publication gates (directional hit rates, vol wins against the nulls, regime grades) are operator-only. Any answer in the language of advice (buy, sell, short, go long, you should, recommend, allocate, position size, take profit, stop loss) is rejected on the server for every caller, the operator included. LIMITS of the citation check: it proves a cited id exists, not that the row supports the sentence, and an id inside a code span counts; the cited rows are shown beside the answer so a reader can check. | `TestCheckCitations`, `TestAdviceIsRejected`, `TestValidateRejects`, `TestMemberCatalogIsImpersonal`, `TestUngatedRecordsAreOperatorOnly` (internal/copilot); `TestAskMemberOffByDefault`, `TestAskMemberCatalogCarriesNoVendorSentinels`, `TestAskOperatorTierNeedsTheAdmin`, `TestAskMemberBudgets`, `TestAskMemberPoolIsAtomic`; `/api/ask` is listed in `memberRoutes`, its one input (`question`) in `memberInputs`. |

## When you add a member feature

1. If it reads a new request parameter, add it to `memberInputs` with what it is.
   If you are tempted to add one that describes the member's money, stop: that is
   the line this file exists to hold.
2. If its body differs per member, it belongs in `perUserRoutes` only if it
   serves the member's own settings or list, never a forecast or a ranking.
3. Any number with "accuracy", "return" or "performance" in it needs its
   hypothetical/backtest label in the same payload or message.
